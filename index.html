Physics Applets for Theoretical Mechanics

Interactive visualizations for abstract physics concepts

Welcome to our collection of physics applets! These interactive tools were developed by Brian Shuve and his research students at Harvey Mudd College to visualize abstract concepts from theoretical mechanics and draw connections to physical intuition from Newtonian mechanics.
